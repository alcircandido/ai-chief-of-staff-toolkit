# Goals

Five goals, one measure each. The chief ranks the list by these: an item that blocks a goal goes first.
Rewrite them as yours. Keep the count low; a goal with no measure cannot rank anything.

| Goal     | What it means                           | The one measure                      |
|----------|-----------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| family   | School and home run with no late forms  | 0 forms or replies past their date   |
| network  | Talk to people worth learning from      | 2 new conversations a week           |
| speaking | Give talks that lead to more talks      | 1 talk booked each quarter           |
| money    | Invoices and bills go out on time       | 0 invoices sent after the 15th       |
| health   | Keep the weekly routine                 | 3 sessions a week on the calendar    |
