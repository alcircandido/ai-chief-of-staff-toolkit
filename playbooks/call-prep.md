# Playbook: one way to prep a call

## When to use it

Each morning, for every call on the calendar in the next 2 days. One short page per call, facts only.

## What good looks like

A page the person reads in two minutes on the way to the call and that changes how the first five minutes go.

## The steps

1. **Who**: name, role, and the one or two facts that matter for this call. Each fact with its source and date.
2. **Why this call**: who asked for it, what was said in the thread, what each side wants from it.
3. **The opener**: one line the person can say first, built on something true and specific (a shared contact, their recent talk, the thread).
4. **Three questions**: the three things worth learning in this call. Questions about their work and their view, in that order.
5. **What not to raise**: anything the rules file or the notebook marks as off limits for this person or this kind of call.
6. **After the call**: the same day, add 3 lines to the notebook below: what was learned, what was promised, what to do next. A promise becomes a row on the list at once.

## Hard rules

- Facts only, each with a source. A guess about the person is left out.
- Short. If it does not fit one phone screen and a half, cut it.
- Read the notebook before any prep: what was learned in earlier calls with this person or people like them.
- Never offer a time, a favor or an introduction in the prep that the person has not approved.

## The check before handing over

- Every fact has a source and a date. Anything older than a year is marked as old.
- The opener is true and could not be said to anyone else.
- Script: the call on the page matches the calendar entry (day, time, zone, who).

## The notebook

One block per call, newest first.

- {YYYY-MM-DD}, {who, by role}: learned {..}; promised {..}; next {..}

## Learned

- {YYYY-MM-DD}: {what the person changed in a prep} -> {the rule it becomes}
